# Galactogobbler — Strict Canon

Canon ID: `boss.level-05.galactogobbler`  
Approved sheet: `../../../reference/characters/level-05/sprites/boss-galactogobbler.png`  
Role: Level 5 boss; gravity and portal controller; Eater of Lost Things

## Identity and silhouette — LOCKED

Galactogobbler is a small purple extraterrestrial protected by a nearly spherical self-assembled shell of compacted asteroid rock, electronics, machinery, and cosmic garbage. The armored silhouette is a dark trash planet with an alien face or glowing core visible through a circular front opening and loose scraps held in orbit. The vulnerable silhouette is a toddler-sized soft purple alien beside or clutching a cyan glowing canister.

Armored scale may shift between gameplay and dramatic promotional presentation, but its spherical proportions and the inner creature's relative smallness are immutable.

## Inner creature — LOCKED

- Skin: smooth lavender through deep violet.
- Head: large, round, bulbous; no hair.
- Eyes: two enormous glossy black-purple oval eyes with bright white highlights.
- Nose: tiny nostril dots.
- Mouth: wide dark oval capable of extreme cavernous stretch; any tiny rounded edge teeth remain subordinate to the maw.
- Body: soft compact torso, short arms, small rounded fingers, short seated/crawling lower body, neck/belly folds.
- Device: cyan-blue luminous cylindrical canister/battery with transparent glowing chamber, dark end caps, and metallic fittings; its blue light illuminates hands and lower face.
- Affect: curious, uncertain, hungry, frightened, and intelligent; never conventionally demonic.

## Planetary shell — LOCKED

The shell is almost round overall but densely irregular in detail. It contains black/dark-brown rocks, corroded machinery, circuit boards, metal panels, pipes, circular speakers/lenses, cameras, small screens, cans, cylinders, bolts, springs, handles, antennae, wire bundles, broken appliances, violet nodules/crystals, and cyan/blue indicator lights.

At front center is a circular aperture that may read as cockpit, eye, emitter, portal, or gravitational core. The alien face remains visible in appropriate states. The large introduction form may use a massive annular trash ring on a metal platform with violet-lit segments.

Glossy black tied bags bearing a violet orbital/cosmic emblem are canonical associated forms. Their exact narrative function—dormant shell, stored matter, disguise, or reconstitution vessel—is **TBD**.

## Motion vocabulary — LOCKED

- Armored idle: components shift; small debris orbits; violet lights pulse; face blinks; sphere subtly settles/rises.
- Roll: whole shell rotates while central aperture tries to track target; pale violet crescents form; debris trails then returns to orbit.
- Charge: rotation and energy rings build; shell rockets forward; collision ejects scraps and gravitational recoil retrieves some.
- Core shot: aperture brightens violet to white-blue; concentrated orb launches with recoil.
- Gravity tether: violet orbs travel on white-purple trails and may pull, curve, orbit, or reverse.
- Meteor control: asteroids drift under control or ignite into ground impacts with orange-yellow cores and black debris.
- Portal: pale violet-white rings become spiral/whirlpool with dark center; bodies or projectiles enter and exit linked apertures.
- Ground collapse: shell sinks into rubble with core exposed, travels as disturbed stones, rebuilds elsewhere.
- Damage: chunks blast away; sphere becomes smaller/rougher; alien/core becomes more exposed.
- Shell destruction: armor becomes a field of rubble, smoke, wire, and parts; alien emerges alive beside canister.
- Bag sequence: alien/core enters a black emblem bag; bag bulges; emblem brightens; matter reassembles or fails.

## Attack canon — CONTROLLED

Allowed structure: Orbital Roll, Core Bolt, Gravity Sling, Meteor Draw, Meteor Drop, Event Horizon, Wormhole Pair, Scrap Satellite, Buried Planet, staged Mass Shedding, Core Exposure, and interruptible Black-Bag Reconstitution. New attacks must derive from gravity, portals, orbiting debris, meteors, shell mass, the canister, or matter assembly.

## Personality, intent, and backstory — INFERRED CANON

Galactogobbler is driven by hunger, fascination, collection, and fear rather than calculated cruelty. It does not reliably distinguish garbage, technology, food, weapons, and living things; all appear as potential shell mass.

It arrived inside a discarded interstellar energy canister. Unable to survive the atmosphere unprotected, it assembled nearby landfill matter into a miniature artificial planet. Absorbed objects strengthened the shell and taught it about Earth. The canister misreads hunger and fear as gravitational commands, so distress causes the surrounding world to fall toward it.

## Visual-effects contract — LOCKED

Core palette is violet, lavender, magenta-white, cyan-blue, and deep near-black. Gravity uses curved trails, rings, orbit paths, pull-distorted debris, and luminous white-violet centers. Meteor impacts add limited orange-yellow heat. Do not replace gravity with green poison, conventional fire magic, or blue lightning as the primary identity.
