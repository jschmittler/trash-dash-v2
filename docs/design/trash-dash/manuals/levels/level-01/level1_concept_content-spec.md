## Footer

### Design notes
- All surfaces are grounded on earth, roots, or supported wood and stone.
- Clear, readable gameplay surfaces with mossy grass tops.
- Strong parallax layers for forest depth and mist.
- Woodland-urban-edge mood with Trash Dash litter personality.

### Material icons (5)
1. **MOSSY EARTH & GRASS** — grass-topped stone platform
2. **WOODEN DEBRIS** — wooden crate / barricade
3. **STONE & CONCRETE** — broken concrete block
4. **FOREST FOLIAGE** — fern clump / mushroom cluster
5. **TRAIL TRASH** — tire pile / rusty can
