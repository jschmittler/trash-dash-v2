## Design intent (user-provided)

Level 3 uses a deliberate **vertical rhythm** across five environment types:

1. Restaurant alley — dumpsters, crates, fire escapes, steam vents, sewer access  
2. Rainy downtown avenue — traffic, scaffolding, awnings, construction barriers, open manholes  
3. Rooftop run — water towers, antennas, gardens, clotheslines, suspended walkways  
4. Subway and maintenance tunnels — platforms, trains, electrified rails, ladders, sewer routes  
5. High-rise construction-site finale — cranes, girders, suspended materials, research tower foreshadowing Level 4  

**Path philosophy (from dynamic layout):** Extreme vertical zig-zag — alley floor → street → rooftops → underground → tallest point in the city.

## Blueprint structure note

Unlike flat levels, this blueprint is a **vertical zig-zag cross-section** read left → right, showing altitude changes explicitly (similar to `level-03-dynamic-layout-blueprint`). The panorama climbs and drops between zones while maintaining the standard 9-zone strip, legend, and boss callout.

**Path overlays:**
- **Blue solid/dashed line** — main intended route (matches dynamic layout blue path)
- **Orange/yellow dashed line** — sewer shortcut through open manhole (lower risk alternate)
- **Green dashed** — shortcut bypass (fire-escape skip on Zone 2–3 transition)
- **Blue dashed (challenge)** — high scaffolding route on Zone 3 with extra tokens

## Panorama — zone-by-zone layout

### Zone 1 — RESTAURANT ALLEY
- **Start:** Trashy far left on wet alley floor.
- **Environment art:** `environment-level-3-01-restaurant-alley`
- **Props:** `level3_dumpster` (×2), `level3_trash_bag_pile`, `level3_milk_crate`, `level3_pizza_boxes`
- **Set dressing:** Steam vent, green sewer ooze, fire escape base, restaurant back door.
- **Enemies:** 1× **Alley Cat Burglar** (low alley patrol).
- **Collectibles:** Trash token intro arc.
- **Verticality:** Low floor — path begins climbing at Zone 2.

### Zone 2 — FIRE ESCAPE ASCENT
- **Environment:** Tiered fire escape landings rising from alley (continuation of env 01).
- **Props:** `level3_milk_crate` stacks as landings, `level3_bench_fragment` on mid landing.
- **Enemies:** 1× **Alley Cat Burglar** (pounce from upper landing).
- **Path overlay:** Green dashed shortcut — skip one landing via dumpster lid hop (`level3_dumpster` standable).
- **Verticality:** Steep climb to street tier.

### Zone 3 — RAINY AVENUE
- **Environment art:** `environment-level-3-02-rainy-downtown-avenue`
- **Props:** `level3_construction_barricade`, `level3_traffic_cones`, `level3_nyc_newspaper_box`, `level3_road_sign_one_way`, `level3_manhole_cover` (open with steam)
- **Power-up:** **Taco Power-Up** on upper scaffolding deck (per dynamic layout).
- **Enemies:** 1× **Traffic-Cone Crab** (scaffolding patrol); 1× **Subway Roach** (under awning cover).
- **Path overlays:** Orange dashed sewer shortcut enters open manhole to Zone 6 lower route; blue challenge route over bus roof with bonus tokens.
- **Verticality:** Street tier — highest local point before rooftop transition.

### Zone 4 — ROOFTOP RUN
- **Environment art:** `environment-level-3-03-rooftop-run`
- **Props:** `level3_bench_fragment`, `level3_cable_spool`, `level3_milk_crate`; rope bridge between roofs.
- **Power-up:** **Kite Power-Up** on high clothesline route (blue challenge path).
- **Enemies:** 1× **Sewer Rat Courier** (rooftop chase lane); 1× **Subway Roach** (cover-to-cover sprint).
- **Set dressing:** Water tower, satellite dish, clothesline, moon.
- **Verticality:** Horizontal sprint then **maintenance shaft drop** into Zone 5.

### Zone 5 — MANHOLE DROP
- **Transition zone:** Elevator/maintenance shaft plunge from rooftop (or orange-route manhole entry from Zone 3).
- **Props:** `level3_sewer_grate`, ladder shaft, `level3_manhole_cover`
- **Hazard:** Shaft fall gap — miss = drop to sewer runoff (bottom hazard band).
- **Enemies:** None (transition beat).
- **Collectibles:** Optional tokens on narrow shaft ledges.

### Zone 6 — SUBWAY TUNNELS
- **Environment art:** `environment-level-3-04-subway-maintenance-tunnels`
- **Props:** `level3_bench_fragment` (platform), `level3_cable_spool`, subway train glow in background
- **Hazard:** **Electrified rails** — blue arc gap (fall/contact hazard); **sewer runoff** channel at bottom.
- **Enemies:** 1× **Subway Roach** (platform cover); 1× **Sewer Rat Courier** (lower lane); 1× **Traffic-Cone Crab** (upper ledge).
- **Path overlay:** Orange route merges from Zone 3 manhole; main route descends then climbs ladders out.

### Zone 7 — CONSTRUCTION CLIMB
- **Environment art:** `environment-level-3-05-construction-site-finale` (ascent portion)
- **Props:** `level3_construction_barricade`, `level3_traffic_cones`, crane cable, suspended pallet
- **Set dressing:** Steel girders, orange safety netting, generator lights; **research tower with satellite dish visible in far background** (Level 4 foreshadow).
- **Enemies:** 1× **Traffic-Cone Crab**; 1× **Alley Cat Burglar** (girder pounce).
- **Verticality:** Ascending climb to boss tier — tallest point in city.

### Zone 8 — SKYLINE FINALE (Boss Arena)
- **Environment art:** `environment-level-3-05-construction-site-finale` (arena platform)
- **Boss:** **His Greasiness, the Pizza Rat King** — three-hit boss in greasy pizza-pan chariot; crown of forks; RAT-1 plate; pizza-cutter scythe; PIZZA IS POWER flag.
- **Health bar:** `HIS GREASINESS, THE PIZZA RAT KING`
- **Enemies:** Boss only.
- **Set dressing:** Flat construction deck; city skyline; research tower glow behind boss.

### Zone 9 — THE DUMPSTER (Goal)
- **Goal object:** Green industrial dumpster with recycling symbol and small green flag — matching Levels 1/2/4/5/6 goal pattern.
- **Props:** `level3_pizza_boxes`, `level3_trash_bag_pile` set dressing beside dumpster.
- **Collectibles:** Final token cluster near goal.
