## Footer

### Design notes
- All surfaces are grounded on stadium structure, dirt, or supported decking.
- Clear readable gameplay surfaces on track, seats, and dugout wood.
- Strong parallax layers for ruined stadium depth at night.
- Post-apocalyptic ballpark mood with Trash Dash litter personality.

### Material icons (5)
1. **CRACKED BRICK & IVY** — cracked brick wall
2. **STADIUM SEATING** — broken seat cluster
3. **DUGOUT WOOD & GEAR** — dugout bench / bat rack
4. **CONCESSIONS CLUTTER** — concessions junk / peanut boxes
5. **WARNING TRACK DIRT** — home plate / infield mud
