# Trash Dash — Strict Canon

Canon ID: `boss.level-01.trash-dash`  
Approved sheet: `../../../reference/characters/level-01/sprites/boss-trash-dash.png`  
Role: Level 1 boss; fast toxic brawler; the Runaway Refuse

## Identity and silhouette — LOCKED

Trash Dash is a sentient, overfilled charcoal-black garbage bag monster. The body is one squat, bottom-heavy sack: a lumpy teardrop or rounded triangle, narrow at the tied top, widest across the lower torso, and flattened directly against the ground. It has no neck, pelvis, or visible legs. It is approximately as wide as tall in its ordinary pose and reads as waist- to chest-high relative to a human, subject to the shared scale policy.

The defining silhouette is the upright tied knot, broad slumped sack, two short arms, embedded face, and continuous slime-covered ground contact. Unless jumping, the lower mass—not feet—supports the body.

## Physical construction — LOCKED

- Material: thin, stretched, wrinkled refuse-bag plastic; charcoal rather than pure black, with slate-gray highlights and near-black creases.
- Top: compressed tied neck with three dominant rounded knot lobes; a fourth overlap may read in some frames. The lobes flop and lag during motion.
- Damage mark: irregular warm ochre-brown tear/patch high on the rear-left shoulder.
- Arms: two short bag-material arms ending in crude mitten hands with about three readable digit or knuckle lobes; no clean anatomical shoulder joint.
- Face: embedded directly in the sack; no separate muzzle and no nose.
- Eyes: two large close-set glowing yellow to yellow-orange eyes with tiny black pupils under a massive permanent V-shaped brow.
- Mouth: broad, low, black interior; thin red gum edge; muted red tongue in open poses.
- Teeth: sparse, mismatched, dirty off-white triangular teeth. Standard snarl reads as approximately 8–10 visible teeth, usually four prominent upper fangs plus four or five lower teeth. Visibility may change with deformation; never regularize them.
- Base residue: glossy chartreuse-green slime, soft garbage lumps, droplets, scraps, and occasional flies or specks.

Trash Dash is never furry, cloth-bodied, humanoid, legged, skeletal, clean, glossy-perfect, or made from a rigid trash can.

## Pose and expression — LOCKED

Neutral posture is a low compressed crouch with the sack spread on the floor, knot upright, fists beside the body, glowing eyes fixed on the target, and teeth exposed. Its emotional baseline is a hostile, pressurized scowl. It may stretch tall only during roars and severe anticipation.

## Motion vocabulary — LOCKED

- Idle: slow inflate/deflate; lower-body lateral bulge; knot bob; slight fist lift; slime tremor or bubble.
- Travel: shuffling sack contractions, crawling stretch, low hops, rolling, or elastic lunges. Forward edge stretches, rear compresses, knot drags backward.
- Roar: body stretches vertically; arms rise or spread; mouth becomes a huge vertical black cavity.
- Toxic spit: mouth retracts/puckers; chartreuse glob begins as a narrow stream, swells into a comet-shaped mass, sheds droplets, and lands as a crown splash then persistent puddle.
- Body slam: compress, launch diagonally, tuck arms, pitch forward, land with radial slime, stones, and ground scar.
- Sludge dive: flatten into slime until face and knot are last visible; travel as a ripple; erupt with an outward splash.
- Stun: body soft and low; unfocused upward eyes; slack arms; yellow stars orbit the knot.
- Rage: eyes turn red; brow lowers; steam puffs; red-orange impact accents intensify.
- Defeat: sack deflates into a small inert gray-black refuse heap, scattered scraps, and leaking green slime.

## Attack canon — CONTROLLED

The boss may use Sludge Spit, persistent Rot Puddles, Garbage Dash, Bag Bounce, Full-Weight Slam, Sludge Dive, Trash Tantrum, and a low-health Red-Eye Rage. New attacks must arise from bag pressure, sludge, bodily weight, refuse debris, or elastic movement. Do not give it conventional handheld weapons, elemental magic unrelated to waste, wings, or technological machinery.

## Personality, intent, and backstory — INFERRED CANON

Trash Dash is instinctive, territorial, irritable, and perpetually uncomfortable. It attacks as if trying to discharge the pressure inside it. Its strategy is blunt: contaminate space, build speed, and crush intruders.

It formed when illegally discarded chemical waste, spoiled food, and experimental fragments fused inside an ordinary sanitation bag under landfill heat and compactor pressure. It tore free as a single metabolic organism. Remaining in motion keeps its unstable contents from settling, so it claims alleys, transfer stations, and drainage tunnels through constant movement and living sludge.

## Visual-effects contract — LOCKED

Primary effect color is glossy chartreuse to yellow-green with darker olive edges. Supporting effects are dirty gray dust, black debris, yellow stun stars, red rage eyes, pale steam, and red-orange maximum-impact accents. Slime behaves as thick elastic fluid, not water, fire, smoke, or magical plasma.

## Forbidden drift

No legs or shoes; no separate head; no friendly round eyes; no neat human teeth; no blue/clear slime; no clean tied bag; no trash-can armor; no cape, crown, horns, ears, tail, or weapon; no ordinary walking cycle.
