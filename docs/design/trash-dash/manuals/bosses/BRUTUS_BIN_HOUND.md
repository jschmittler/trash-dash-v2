# Brutus Bin Hound — Strict Canon

Canon ID: `boss.level-02.brutus-bin-hound`  
Approved sheet: `../../../reference/characters/level-02/sprites/boss-brutus-bin-hound.png`  
Role: Level 2 boss; territorial armored bulldog; Guardian of the Garbage Run

## Identity and silhouette — LOCKED

Brutus is an oversized tan-orange English bulldog on four short legs, wearing a large overturned wheeled trash bin horizontally over his torso as armor. The immutable silhouette is a huge wrinkled bulldog head protruding from the forward opening, four paws beneath the bin, a tiny rear tail, and two black wheels sitting on top because the container is inverted.

He is larger than a normal bulldog. The sheet's child comparison places the head around a child's chest and the bin near shoulder height, but exact measurements remain unlocked.

## Physical construction — LOCKED

- Body: broad chest, compact hips, short thick legs, oversized paws with pale claws, stubby curled tail.
- Coat: tan to ochre with darker fold shadows; cream muzzle, chin, chest, and toes.
- Head: enormous square skull; small folded rose ears; deep forehead and cheek wrinkles; heavy brow; narrow dark eyes; large black nose; central crease above nose.
- Mouth: heavy drooping jowls and a pronounced underbite. Two lower canine teeth commonly project upward while closed.
- Open mouth: red interior and tongue; approximately 8–12 visible blunt triangular teeth, with four especially prominent canines. Visibility varies by pose; preserve the bulldog bite pattern.
- Collar: broad bright red-orange leather collar, dark edging, evenly spaced silver cone studs.
- Armor: scratched dark blue-gray galvanized wheeled trash bin, rectangular tapered body, vertical stamped ribs, pale scuffs, heavy dark rims, head through forward opening, legs beneath lower rim.
- Wheels: two black wheels with gray spiral hubs on the upper silhouette.
- Lid: hinged or loose bin lid can flap, open, slide, or cover the head; Brutus can retract fully into the shell.

Brutus is an English bulldog, not a mastiff, pit bull, hound-shaped dog, robot dog, or dog-headed trash can.

## Pose and expression — LOCKED

Neutral pose is a low square four-legged stance facing right. The head projects forward, brow lowers, and underbite shows. Baseline emotion is annoyed, tired vigilance—not constant frenzy. He can doze while standing guard, shown by closed eyes and small sleep symbols.

## Motion vocabulary — LOCKED

- Idle: heavy breathing through chest and jowls; ear twitch; eye glance; slight bin rock; wheel twitch; optional standing sleep.
- Walk: short stiff steps; heavy shell bobs minimally.
- Bark: head draws back then thrusts forward; jaw opens nearly vertically; jowls stretch; ears flatten; sound lines burst outward.
- Sprint: body leans very low; legs extend; dust and speed strokes trail; loose bin shifts backward.
- Bite rush: mouth open, teeth exposed, red head-level speed slashes.
- Chin plow: head/chest scrape the ground while bin rides over him, creating furrows, dust, and possible sparks.
- Stun: head droops; eyes unfocus; yellow stars circle above brow/bin.
- Shell retreat: head and legs withdraw; lid closes; bin may hop, slide, or roll.
- Rolling bin: closed container rotates horizontally; damaged form bursts open and spills garbage.
- Water reaction: hydrant or fountain water soaks fur and armor; droplets cling; water interrupts, exposes, cleans, or weakens him.
- Dig: forelegs brace while hind legs kick dirt into a mound.
- Defeat: non-graphic exhaustion, soaking, stun, pacification, or separation from armor; do not depict gore.

## Attack canon — CONTROLLED

Allowed structure: Warning Bark, Sonic Howl, Bin Charge, Bite Charge, Chin Plow, Shell Retreat, Wheel Roll, Trash Spill, Dig and Ambush, hydrant/fountain hazards, Bath-Time Weakness, and Last Bark combinations. New attacks must derive from bulldog behavior, barking, biting, digging, bodily momentum, bin armor, trash spillage, or arena water.

## Personality, intent, and backstory — INFERRED CANON

Brutus is territorial, loyal, stubborn, sleep-deprived, and defensive rather than evil. He warns intruders, escalates when ignored, and becomes anxious or embarrassed without his shell.

He once belonged to a sanitation worker and rode with the collection crew. After the yard was abandoned, he kept guarding the bins while waiting for a driver who never returned. A damaged wheeled container became shelter, then armor. Years of chasing scavengers made every discarded object feel like his responsibility. Kiddie pools and water recall his gentler former life and can pacify or expose him.

## Visual-effects contract — LOCKED

Dust is warm beige-gray; charge accents may turn red; stun uses bright yellow stars; water is clean cyan-blue with white highlights; dirt is medium brown; refuse hazards retain recognizable trash colors. Bark effects are graphic lines or compressed air, not fire breath or magic beams.
