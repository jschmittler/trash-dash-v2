# The Diamond Don — Strict Canon

Canon ID: `boss.level-06-secret.diamond-don`  
Approved sheet: `../../../reference/characters/level-06/sprites/boss-diamond-don.png`  
Canonical display name: **The Diamond Don**; “Diamond Don” is acceptable in dialogue and filenames  
Role: Secret Level 6 boss; raccoon kingpin, baseball bruiser, cursed-power enforcer

## Identity and silhouette — LOCKED

The Diamond Don is a towering upright anthropomorphic raccoon with a heavyweight prizefighter build, an enormous ringed tail, low black fedora, dirty baseball/gangster clothing, gold chains, and a huge reinforced spiked wooden bat. He is broad, belly-heavy, powerful, and approximately 6.5–7.5 feet in presentation, subject to final scale chart approval.

The defining silhouette is upright and wide: fedora, massive shoulders and fists, open dirty shirt, bat, and full black-gray tail sweeping behind.

## Physical construction — LOCKED

- Fur: charcoal-black/deep gray with black eye mask, lighter gray muzzle/cheeks, small rounded dark ears.
- Face: pale yellow-green/gray reflective eyes, broad black nose, wide mouth, small sharp teeth, smug crooked grin.
- Tail: extremely full with alternating black and gray rings; curves outward and materially enlarges silhouette.
- Build: thick barrel chest, round belly, wide shoulders, huge forearms/hands, short powerful legs, plantigrade stance.
- Hat: low black brimmed fedora/trilby.
- Outer shirt: dirty off-white/gray short-sleeved baseball shirt with dark piping/fine vertical striping, torn/stained/strained.
- Sleeve mark: distressed dark two-digit number reading most like `06`; preserve the visual mark and its ambiguity rather than substituting a new number.
- Underlayer: black/charcoal armored vest or shirt.
- Accessories: multiple dark belts and brown straps across belly, gold buckles, heavy gold neck chains.
- Lower clothing: dark trousers, heavy black boots.
- Hands: fingerless gloves/armored wraps; one forearm may carry a reinforced metal gauntlet with subtle green indicators.
- Bat: enormous dark-brown wooden club/baseball bat with wrapped grip, metal bands, numerous short spikes/studs, dents, scratches, and stains; usable as two-handed maul.
- Associated props: dirty off-white red-stitched baseball, gold coin stacks, thick gold chain with padlock.

## Pose and expression — LOCKED

Neutral stance is upright, feet wide, torso slightly back to balance belly and weapon. One massive guarded fist hangs forward while the other supports the bat. Expression is smug, controlled, predatory, and confrontational. Pointing is a signature command gesture.

## Motion vocabulary — LOCKED

- Idle: shoulder rise, tail sway, chain bounce, low hat, grip adjustment, fist clench.
- Walk: swagger with shoulder rotation; run becomes stomping forward lean with dust and trailing tail/shirt.
- Command: thick finger points decisively at target or minions.
- Pitch: exaggerated shoulder windup; red-stitched baseball launches with pale speed lines and may ricochet.
- Bat sweep: planted feet, hip rotation, broad dark motion crescent.
- Overhead slam: bat raised high then driven down with full mass, producing gray-brown shockwave, stones, and linear ground crack.
- Weapon drop/hazard: oversized club lands into ground and throws rubble.
- Spin: body tucks into a black-gray circular vortex; tail, shirt, and weapon blur.
- Curse phase: violet smoke, green-violet spectral skull/symbol forms, darker saturated silhouette, summoned raccoon figures or mines.
- Stun: yellow stars, tilted hat, twisted torso, lifted leg; violet sparks when cursed.
- Defeat: falls side/stomach; hat and bat separate; tail goes limp; may be partially buried or seated exhausted; no gore.

## Attack canon — CONTROLLED

Allowed structure: Collection Swing, Foreclosure Slam, Beanball, Ricochet Pitch, Kingpin Spin, Point of Order, Protection Racket, Diamond Curse, spectral Skull Mines, and Last Collection combinations. New attacks must derive from bat/club combat, baseball pitching, body mass, criminal command, minions, jewelry/wealth props, or the established violet-green curse.

## Personality, intent, and backstory — INFERRED CANON

Don is calculating, arrogant, theatrical, possessive, status-conscious, and accustomed to obedience. He treats battle as debt collection: warning, demonstration, punishment. Humiliation breaks his control.

He controlled night crews that stripped valuables from refuse and built a black market around salvage, abandoned jewelry, and rigged street games. His name comes from a flawless stone he claims is hidden inside the padlock on his chain. Cosmic material discarded after Galactogobbler's passage infused his collection with violet energy, producing the cursed phase and intensifying his paranoia about theft.

## Visual-effects contract — LOCKED

Physical impacts use dirty gray/brown shockwaves, rubble, black arcs, pale baseball streaks, and yellow stun stars. Supernatural effects use smoky violet with acid-green accents and skull-like circular motifs. Gold remains material jewelry/coins, not clean holy magic. Green indicators on the gauntlet remain subtle.
