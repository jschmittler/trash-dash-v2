# Pizza Rat King — Strict Canon

Canon ID: `boss.level-03.pizza-rat-king`  
Approved sheet: `../../../reference/characters/level-03/sprites/boss-pizza-rat-king.png`  
Role: Level 3 boss; sewer monarch, cutter bruiser, food projectile attacker, rat summoner

## Identity and silhouette — LOCKED

The Pizza Rat King is an enormous obese sewer rat, boar-sized in combat presentation, with greasy dark brown-gray fur, a broad dirty pale belly, a long naked pink segmented tail, a battered fork crown, a ragged red-brown mantle, and an oversized circular pizza cutter. The defining silhouette is a low mountainous rat body crowned by bent fork tines and fronted by a silver wheel.

## Physical construction — LOCKED

- Body: heavy mound, hunched back, wide hips, short limbs, belly near ground.
- Fur: soot brown, dark olive-gray, near-black shadow, reddish-brown spiky tufts; greasy and coarse, never plush.
- Contamination: orange grease flecks, yellow crumbs, sauce-like stains.
- Head: long angular rat muzzle, rounded ears partly hidden by crown/mane, thick brows, pink nose, long sparse whiskers.
- Eyes: large round pale yellow eyes with tiny dark pupils.
- Mouth: broad dark interior, red tongue, two dominant stained yellow rat incisors plus approximately 6–10 smaller pointed teeth visible in large open poses.
- Limbs: pink clawed forepaws and hind feet.
- Tail: long, naked, pink, visibly segmented, tapering to a narrow point.
- Crown: dented metal band worn low; multiple bent forks rise as irregular dark purple-black royal prongs; asymmetrical, greasy, damaged, improvised.
- Mantle: shredded red-brown layer across shoulders and back, reading as sauce-stained fabric, hardened pizza, or refuse; long trailing strips during motion.
- Weapon: huge dirty silver-gray circular pizza cutter with central hub, concentric scuffs, grease marks, straight gray axle/handle; wheel reaches approximately ground-to-chest.
- Pizza projectile: triangular slice with browned crust, red sauce, yellow cheese, and dark toppings.

## Pose and expression — LOCKED

Neutral posture is seated or deeply crouched, belly close to ground, one forepaw controlling the cutter. Expression is greedy, taunting, imperious, and open-mouthed. Upright seated gestures are allowed for commands and throws; ordinary locomotion remains low.

## Motion vocabulary — LOCKED

- Idle: heavy belly breathing; nose twitch; food-seeking eye dart; cutter wobble; crown-fork quiver; falling crumbs.
- Walk: waddles/scurries while pushing or rolling cutter; shoulders rise; tail counterbalances.
- Cutter charge: head low, wheel leading, mantle/whiskers/fur trailing.
- Maximum rush: body stretches horizontally; paws blur; mane becomes speed streaks; wheel remains a crisp leading circle.
- Pizza throw: rises seated, holds slice, draws paw back, releases spinning/tumbling slice with crumbs and cheese.
- Cutter throw: blade separates and rolls or flies through several rotational angles; return path is permitted.
- Hit/stagger: pale belly exposed; yellow impact flash; body reels; crown tilts.
- Stun: yellow stars; arms spread; eyes wide or unfocused.
- Summon: smaller gray rats enter; they retain rat anatomy and lack the king's size, mantle, crown, and weapon.
- Defeat: collapses forward or sideways; crown and cutter separate; mound flattens; tail extends; no gore.

## Attack canon — CONTROLLED

Allowed structure: Royal Cutter, Rolling Decree, Slice Toss, Pizza Barrage, Grease Rush, Supreme Delivery, Call the Court, interruptible Feast Recovery, Crown Tantrum, and a Dethroned phase after crown/cutter loss. New attacks must derive from the cutter, thrown pizza, grease/crumbs, rat movement, appetite, crown authority, or summoned rats.

## Personality, intent, and backstory — INFERRED CANON

The king is gluttonous, theatrical, vain, possessive, commanding, and genuinely dangerous. He treats discarded food as royal tribute and sewer rats as subjects. Touching his food or crown turns smug performance into rage.

He began as a subway rat that found a restaurant's discarded ceremonial pizza cutter and adopted a broken utensil rack as a crown. Other rats brought him slices; generations of tribute, chemical runoff, and constant feeding enlarged him. He now taxes delivery routes and regards every untouched pizza as property of the crown.

The suggested personal name “King Mozzarellus” is optional flavor, not a replacement for the locked public name Pizza Rat King.

## Visual-effects contract — LOCKED

Effects center on dirty beige dust, yellow cheese stretches and crumbs, orange grease, red sauce flecks, silver wheel arcs, white speed streaks, yellow impact stars, and brown rubble. Do not turn pizza effects into generic fire or the crown into clean gold magic.

## Forbidden drift

No jeweled gold crown; no chef hat; no clean cape; no normal sword; no small slender rat body; no furry tail; no human hands; no clean white teeth; no pizza-box armor; no removal of fork crown or cutter except in explicitly dethroned/defeat states.
