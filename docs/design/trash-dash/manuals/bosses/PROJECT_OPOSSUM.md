# Project O.P.O.S.S.U.M. — Strict Canon

Canon ID: `boss.level-04.project-opossum`  
Approved sheet: `../../../reference/characters/level-04/sprites/boss-project-opossum.png`  
Canonical display name: **Project O.P.O.S.S.U.M.**; plain “Project Opossum” is acceptable in filenames and informal prose  
Role: Level 4 boss; cybernetic ambusher, gravity/electricity system, sympathetic host animal

## Identity and silhouette — LOCKED

Project O.P.O.S.S.U.M. is a giant low-slung opossum encumbered by a dense improvised cybernetic laboratory harness. The immutable silhouette combines a long pale pointed face, charcoal quadrupedal body, curled naked ringed tail, and a jagged machine mass covering almost the entire back. It is roughly large-dog to hog sized, subject to the scale policy.

## Physical construction — LOCKED

- Body: long, broad, low quadruped; belly close to ground; coarse charcoal-black/deep-gray fur with irregular tufts.
- Head: pale gray-white sharply tapered face; dark eye patches; small round naked ears with dark rims and pink interiors; long mouth line.
- Eyes: tiny dark eyes that may reflect or glow red in aggression.
- Nose: small pink nose at snout tip; sparse whiskers.
- Teeth: small sharp teeth, exposed only when lips part; never a large carnivore jaw.
- Feet: pink, mostly hairless, narrow multiple toes, small curved claws.
- Tail: long naked pink-purple tail, dark transverse rings, thick base tapering steadily; commonly curls into a hook/question mark and drags limp when exhausted.
- Rig: chaotic black/gunmetal plates, circular housings, blue-lit energy cells, red warning lights, copper coils, exposed red/blue wire, tubing, clamps, straps, rivets, switches, patched panels, large shoulder/hip mechanisms, aerials, sparks.
- Beacon: prominent rotating/flashing red emergency beacon at the top of the harness.
- Construction quality: improvised laboratory prototype, not sleek sci-fi armor.

## Pose and expression — LOCKED

Neutral posture stays on four feet with arched spine, head reaching forward, belly low, and tail curled behind. The animal reads wary, anxious, intelligent, and overstimulated. Emotional cues use ear position, eye width, muzzle tension, tail curl, and machinery state.

## Motion vocabulary — LOCKED

- Idle: nose/whisker twitch; short sniff; tail curl/tap; asynchronous rig vibration; blue pulses; red blink; beacon rotation; intermittent sparks.
- Scuttle: short rapid steps; torso undulates; heavy rig lags.
- Sprint: belly nearly skims floor; head far forward; tail streams back; white-gray dust and horizontal streaks.
- Energy-barrier collision: muzzle/paws strike a tall technological panel; cyan electrical splash; body compresses then recoils.
- Device deployment: small dark spherical mine or instrument released near the rear/tail apparatus.
- Phase cloak: body and rig become cyan-blue semi-transparent with several offset duplicates.
- Gravity portal: violet concentric rings build into a spiral tunnel; dense dark orb and debris pass through.
- Alert/overload: red alert symbol, intense lights, smoke, and sparks; animal appears alarmed.
- Stun: belly collapse, slack tail, yellow stars, smoking components.
- Playing possum: rolls onto side/back with feet slack, open mouth, possible tongue, tail flat; may be authentic collapse or feint.
- Armor loss: biological opossum appears smaller and vulnerable without rig.
- Defeat: de-armored animal lies or rests non-graphically with tail extended.

## Attack canon — CONTROLLED

Allowed structure: Servo Scuttle, Overclocked Charge, Arc Collision, Tail-Deployed Mine, Phase Shift, Gravity Aperture, Singularity Pull, System Overload, Playing Possum feint, and de-armored evasive phase. New attacks must arise from the rig's electricity, gravity, portals, mines, phase displacement, or natural opossum movement. The biological animal does not cast magic independently.

## Personality, intent, and backstory — INFERRED CANON

The host animal is cautious, intelligent, trapped, and primarily defensive; the rig is the aggressive system. Preserve this moral distinction.

A city scavenger was captured by a private waste-recovery laboratory and fitted with a prototype anomalous-refuse retrieval rig because of its natural toxin resistance. The system learned to classify the entire city as contaminated material and began forcing retrieval or elimination behavior. The red beacon is the laboratory's last functioning warning system, not decoration.

The acronym expansion is **TBD**. Do not invent an official expansion without approval.

## Visual-effects contract — LOCKED

Technology light is electric cyan/blue and warning red; gravity/portal energy is luminous violet-magenta with white cores; dust is pale gray; overload smoke is gray-black; stun uses yellow stars. Phase effects preserve a readable cyan opossum silhouette. Never recolor the natural animal purple to imply magic.
