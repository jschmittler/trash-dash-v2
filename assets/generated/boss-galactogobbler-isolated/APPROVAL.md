# Galactogobbler Artwork Approval

Status: **ARTWORK APPROVED**

The project owner approved the Level 5 Galactogobbler isolated artwork package on 2026-08-13.

Approval covers the complete transparent sheet, 224 extracted frames and support/effect sprites, manifest-defined variable rectangles and pivots, boundary-labeled contact sheet, and the approved-only EMERGE, RETREAT, and DEFEAT sequences.

Runtime promotion, engine registration, gameplay integration, collision, timing, encounter changes, real-runtime traversal, and the V2 release gate remain separate work and are not authorized by this approval.
