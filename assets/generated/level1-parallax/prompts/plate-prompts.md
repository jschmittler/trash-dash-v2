# Level 1 plate prompts and ownership

All requests use a matching approved Level 1 canonical background only as visual-direction reference. They must preserve Trash Dash's polished late-16-bit side-scroller art language: crisp pixel clusters, dark blue-gray contours, limited value groups, readable silhouette, and no text, logo, watermark, character, UI, gameplay surface, platform, or route.

| Stage | Far ownership | Middle ownership | Close ownership |
|---|---|---|---|
| deep-woodland | forest silhouettes, fog, canopy | tree trunks, shrubs, mist ribbons | broken signpost, crate, camp-junk fragments |
| creek-and-ruined-mill | misty pines, distant light rays, mill silhouette | waterwheel shell, ruined bridge fragments, tree roots | mossy stump, concrete block, tire-pile fragments |
| forest-edge-highway | distant treeline, far highway line, skyline glimpse | guardrail runs, fence posts, culvert mouths | fallen log fragment, stone chunk, roadside litter |
| industrial-city-fringe | warehouse/rail-yard silhouette, hazy city glow | rail cars, warehouse façade fragments, shipping containers | pallet edge, drain-channel fragment, container corner |
| urban-park-transition | park trees, distant brick-building silhouette, moonlit transition | mature trees, park fencing, distant brick façades | hedge/bench fragment, trash bin, small fence corner |

Far prompt suffix: `opaque full-frame distant vista; quiet matching material at left and right edges; no foreground objects, floor, ledge, platform, route, or collision geometry.`

Middle prompt suffix: `solid #FF00FF external key backdrop; separated middle-depth landmarks only; at least 35% clear key background; no object touches a canvas edge; no floor, route, platform, or character.`

Close prompt suffix: `solid #FF00FF external key backdrop; only 4–7 disconnected edge-weighted non-playable dressing fragments; preserve a clear center and bottom travel corridor; at least 60% clear key background; no continuous floor, ledge, platform, route, or character.`
