# Level 2 plate prompts

All plates use the approved Level 2 references as visual direction: polished late-16-bit side-scroller night-suburb art, deep blue moonlight, warm window/streetlamp accents, and no characters, UI, words, logos, HUD, gameplay floor, collision geometry, platforms, pickups, or hazards.

For each stage, generate one opaque far vista and two isolated moving plates on a flat pure `#FF00FF` key canvas. The moving plates must contain only non-continuous scenic silhouettes/effects and generous clear space around their contours.

| Stage | Far plane | Middle / close silhouettes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| moonlit-backyard | moon, cloud bands, distant rooflines and tree canopy | fence/tree/shed silhouette; shrubs, fireflies, scattered garden objects |
| garbage-night-street | receding neighborhood lights, distant houses, wet-night sky | utility poles and hedge silhouettes; isolated bins, bags, mailbox, foreground leaves |
| backyard-obstacle-course | moonlit roofline and deep treetops | treehouse/fence/laundry silhouette; sprinklers, toys, pool rim, foliage clusters |
| drainage-ditch-and-culvert | distant city glow, night clouds, tree line | culvert/fence/embankment silhouettes; reeds, rocks, fireflies, water-edge accents |
| suburban-main-street | distant skyline and midnight clouds | rooftop/water-tower/telephone silhouettes; storefront awning, lamp, loose rooftop clutter |
